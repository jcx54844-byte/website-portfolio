04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
take a look.

Is Relay a real software service?

No. This is an independent portfolio concept showing product communication, responsive design and a small working interaction.

What can I do in the demo?

Advance the three example tasks from To do to In review to Done. Counts and completion update automatically. Reset restores the starting board.

Will my changes be saved?

No. The demo uses page memory only. Nothing is sent to a server or saved to your device. Refreshing the page restores the sample tasks.

Can I sign up or buy a plan?

No. Prices demonstrate a billing-period toggle. There is no checkout, signup or live subscription.
