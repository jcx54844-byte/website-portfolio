Which clean should I choose?

Regular cleaning covers everyday upkeep. Deep cleaning adds more detailed attention. Moving cleaning is designed around an empty property. Select a service to see its sample checklist.

Are supplies and equipment included?

For a real booking, supplies, equipment, surface requirements and product preferences would be confirmed before a quote. This concept does not promise a particular product or equipment package.

How much does a clean cost?

This concept has no live prices. A real quote would depend on property size, condition and the agreed tasks. The demo below shows the information needed to begin that conversation.

Can I book or send a message here?

No. Clearhaven is a fictional portfolio project. The form demonstrates validation and an enquiry summary in your browser. Nothing is sent, booked or stored.
